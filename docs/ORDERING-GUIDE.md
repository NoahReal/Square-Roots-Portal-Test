# Ordering by the box: how to use it

A short guide for each person involved, and how to switch a route over from the spreadsheets.
Each section can be sent to that person on its own.

---

## For the operations team

**Every cycle, in the partner portal (log in, then use the menu):**

1. **Friday: price lists.** Open **More → Price Lists** and pick the drop. Suppliers with a portal
   account send their own; for the others, open the spreadsheet they sent, select the rows, copy, and
   paste them in (or choose a CSV file). The screen shows what went up ▲, down ▼, is new, or is gone
   since last time.
2. **Build the form.** Open **Order Forms → Start a form**. Pick the drop and the routes (Halifax and
   Halifax North share one form), and keep "Start from the last form" ticked: last form's items come
   across at this drop's prices, and anything that got more expensive is marked. Add or remove items,
   mark good deals ★, and write a note to locations if you like. Check the dates: they come from each
   route's days (see **More → Routes**).
3. **Open it.** Press **Open for orders**. Each location's Community Managers get an email with the
   link (you can also paste the link `/portal/manager/order` into Messenger). This also sets each
   location's market day (the day after the trucks) and when customers can reserve until.
4. **Watch orders come in.** The form shows who has ordered and who hasn't. Community Managers get
   a reminder the day before ordering closes if they haven't.
5. **Send it.** After the deadline, press **Send to suppliers and trucks**. Each supplier gets a
   link showing what they need to send (Ketty Brow's sees totals for each drop-off point; farms see
   each location's order), and each route's trucks get their stops. Anyone without an email on file:
   use **Copy the confirmation link** and send it however you normally would.
6. **Check confirmations.** Each one shows ✓ Confirmed, ✗ Can't do it (with their note), or Waiting.
7. **Trucks day.** **Print route sheets** on the form for the drivers.

**Also:** **Download every order (CSV)** opens in Excel, for Google Drive or SharePoint. Hub shares
show on the form once orders are in.

## For Community Managers

1. You'll get an email (or a Messenger link) when the order form is open.
2. Log in and open **Order**. Choose how many boxes of each item with **−** and **+**. You'll see the
   price per box, how many are available, good deals ★, and what you ordered last time. Your total
   is at the bottom.
3. Press **Save my order**. You can change it until the deadline shown at the top.
4. Your market is usually the day after the trucks come. Customers can reserve until ordering closes.

## For the Fairview hub

Open **Home → Your hub**. For each delivery you'll see every location you take deliveries for, the
boxes of each item that are theirs, and a checklist to tick off as you sort. Your share for sorting
is at the top of each delivery.

## For suppliers (Ketty Brow's, Footes and others)

- **Sending your price list:** if you have a portal account, open **Price List**, pick the drop, and
  paste the rows from your spreadsheet (product, box size, price per box, boxes available). If not,
  keep emailing it to the team as usual.
- **Confirming an order:** you'll get an email with a link. It shows exactly what's needed and where
  it goes. Press **Yes, confirmed**, or **I can't do all of it** and say what you can do. No login.

## For transport companies

You'll get an email with a link for each run: the stops and how many boxes go to each. Press
**Yes, confirmed**, or tell us what you can't do. No login.

## Switching a route over

Each route switches on its own, so nothing breaks for the others.

1. **Before:** on **More → Routes**, check the route's days and suppliers, that every location on it
   has the right drop-off point (set on **More → Locations**), and set who drives its trucks.
   Add the suppliers' email addresses so confirmation links reach them.
2. **Switch it on:** tick **Orders from order forms** for the route. Its Community Managers' Order
   screen changes to the order form straight away.
3. **Run both for two cycles:** keep the spreadsheet going alongside. Compare the totals the portal
   sends with the spreadsheet's.
4. **Retire the spreadsheet** for that route once two cycles in a row match and nobody needed it.
5. **Then the next route.**
